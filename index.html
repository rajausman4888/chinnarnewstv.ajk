Raja Habib Kashmiri

Founder & Owner - Chinnar News TV

Raja Habib Kashmiri is the founder and owner of Chinnar News TV. He is a well-known personality from Kashmir who has worked in multiple fields including media, tourism and television production.

He has served in the Pakistani entertainment industry as a Line Producer, Production Manager and has been involved in several successful drama productions. Some of the most popular projects include Parizaad, Ehd-e-Wafa, Duniya Pur and many more.And also work in ISPR.

He has also worked on OSP (On-location shoots) and contributed to productions related to the Pakistani National Tarana.

Apart from media, he played an important role in promoting tourism in Kashmir by founding Heaven Trackers, one of the earliest tourism companies in the region.

His vision through Chinnar News TV is to raise the voice of Kashmir and highlight its culture, beauty, and real issues globally.
